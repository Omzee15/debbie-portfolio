04 / About me

Curious, mostly.

I learn by building. If something on the internet makes me say "how did they do that", I'm already opening a new project to find out.

I care about the feel of things: how a button lands, how a page moves, whether anyone smiled while using it.

05 / Process

Four moves, no magic.

  1. PokeAsk questions until the real problem shows up.
  2. SketchGet a rough version running as early as possible.
  3. PlayPut it in people's hands and watch what they do.
  4. ShipPolish what matters, cut the rest, send it.
